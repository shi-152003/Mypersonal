ENGLISH GRAMMAR • SHORT NOTES • CHAPTER 1 ## **1. Basic Grammar** Grammar Rules used to speak and write a language correctly. Word A group of letters having meaning. Example: Book, Run, Beautiful Sentence A group of words that gives complete meaning. Example: I am a student. ## **2. Parts of Speech (8)** | **Part of Speech** | **Meaning + Example** | | ------------------ | ------------------------- | | Noun | Name: Ram, Book | | Pronoun | Replaces noun: He, She | | Verb | Action/state: Run, Is | | Adjective | Describes noun: Beautiful | | Adverb | Modifies verb: Slowly | | Preposition | Relation: In, On, At | | Conjunction | Joins: And, But | | Interjection | Feeling: Wow!, Alas! | ## **3. Noun** A noun is the name of a person, place, animal, thing or idea. Types of Nouns | Proper | Special name – Rahul, India | | ---------- | ------------------------------ | | Common | General name – Boy, City | | Collective | Group – Team, Army | | Material | Substance – Gold, Wood | | Abstract | Quality/idea – Honesty, Beauty | ## **4. Countable & Uncountable Nouns** Countable Nouns Can be counted. Book → Books; Pen → Pens Used with: a/an, many, few, several. Uncountable Nouns Generally cannot be counted individually. Advice, Information, Furniture, Water, Knowledge Used with: much, little, some. **Important:** Uncountable nouns generally take singular verbs. The information is useful. The informations are useful. ## **5. Plural Formation Rules** | General | Book → Books | | ------------- | -------------- | | s, sh, ch, x | Box → Boxes | | Consonant + y | City → Cities | | Vowel + y | Boy → Boys | | Some f/fe | Knife → Knives | Irregular Plurals Man → Men | Woman → Women Child → Children | Tooth → Teeth Foot → Feet | Mouse → Mice Goose → Geese | Ox → Oxen Same Singular & Plural Sheep, Deer, Fish, Species, Series ## **6. Important Exam Rules** Rule 1: One of + plural noun + singular verb One of my friends is honest. Rule 2: Each / Every + singular noun + singular verb Every student is present. Rule 3: Many a + singular noun + singular verb Many a student has failed. Rule 4: A number of + plural verb A number of students are absent. Rule 5: The number of + singular verb The number of students is increasing. Rule 6: Plural-only nouns Scissors, Trousers, Spectacles, Goods My trousers are new. A pair of scissors is on the table. ## **7. Quick Revision – Must Remember** - Advice → a piece of advice - Information → much information - Furniture → furniture (not furnitures) - One of my friends **is** - Every student **has** - Many a student **has** - A number of students **are** - The number of students **is** - My trousers **are** - A pair of trousers **is** **Revision tip:** Read these notes once daily for 5 minutes.
