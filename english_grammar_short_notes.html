English Grammar: Short Revision Notes

Competitive Exams • Beginner Grammar • Parts of Speech & Nouns

1. Grammar, Word & Sentence

Grammar: Rules used to speak and write a language correctly.

Word: A group of letters with meaning. Examples: book, run, beautiful.

Sentence: A group of words that gives complete meaning.

Example: She is reading a book.

A sentence usually begins with a capital letter and ends with a full stop (.), question mark (?) or exclamation mark (!).

3. Noun

Definition: A noun is the name of a person, place, animal, thing, quality, feeling or idea.

Examples: teacher, Indore, dog, pen, honesty, happiness.

Five common types of nouns

Proper noun tip: Proper nouns begin with a capital letter. Example: India, Monday, Riya.

4. Countable & Uncountable Nouns

Countable

Can be counted individually.

Examples: one pen, two pens; one apple, three apples.

Use: a/an, many, few, several

Uncountable

Not usually counted individually.

Examples: water, rice, advice, information.

Use: much, little, some

Common uncountable nouns (exam list)

advice, information, furniture, luggage, baggage, knowledge, equipment, scenery, poetry, machinery.

Remember: Uncountable nouns generally do not take a/an or plural -s, and usually take a singular verb.

5. Singular & Plural Nouns

Singular: one item — book, child.

Plural: more than one — books, children.

RuleExamples
Usually add -sbook → books; pen → pens
Endings s, ss, sh, ch, x, z: usually add -esbus → buses; class → classes; box → boxes; watch → watches
Consonant + y: change y to -iescity → cities; baby → babies
Vowel + y: add -sboy → boys; key → keys
Some f/fe nouns change to -vesknife → knives; wife → wives; leaf → leaves
Some f nouns simply add -sroof → roofs; chief → chiefs; belief → beliefs

6. Nouns Usually Used in Plural

scissors, trousers, shorts, spectacles, clothes, goods.

Examples: My trousers are new. / These scissors are sharp.

A pair of + plural-noun form
A pair of scissors is on the table.
Two pairs of scissors are on the table.

7. Important Noun Rules for Competitive Exams

Rule 1One of + plural noun + singular verb
One of my friends is a doctor.
Rule 2Each / Every + singular noun + singular verb
Each student is present.
Every boy has a book.
Rule 3Many a + singular noun + singular verb
Many a student has made this mistake.
Rule 4A number of + plural noun + plural verb
A number of students are absent.
Meaning: many students
Rule 5The number of + plural noun + singular verb
The number of students is increasing.
Meaning: the total count

9. Last-Minute Memory Sheet

  • Noun = name of a person, place, thing, quality or idea.
  • There are 8 Parts of Speech.
  • Advice, information, furniture, luggage and equipment are uncountable.
  • Uncountable nouns generally take singular verbs.
  • One of + plural noun + singular verb.
  • Each/Every + singular noun + singular verb.
  • Many a + singular noun + singular verb.
  • A number of + plural verb; The number of + singular verb.
  • Scissors/trousers are plural; a pair of scissors is singular.
